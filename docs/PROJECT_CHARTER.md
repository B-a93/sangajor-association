# Project Legacy Charter

## Purpose
Project Legacy is the long-term digital development initiative of the SANGAJOR B.C.S Class of 2008 Association. It is designed to strengthen communication, preserve the class's history, support transparent governance, and create a scalable digital foundation.

## Mission
To build a secure, modern and sustainable digital platform that unites members, improves communication, preserves the association's history and supports future community development.

## Vision
To become one of The Gambia's most organised and digitally empowered alumni associations.

## Guiding Principles
- Strengthen member connection
- Improve communication
- Preserve history and documents
- Increase transparency
- Support long-term growth
- Remain authentic and avoid unsupported claims

## Governance
- Chairman: project sponsor and final approval
- Executive Committee: strategic review and policy decisions
- Secretary General: official content and documentation
- Treasurer: financial and donation-related content
- IPRO: product owner, branding, communication and testing
- Elegant Empire AI Studio: design, development and technical support

## Definition of Done
A feature is complete when it is functional, mobile-responsive, accessible, reviewed for accuracy, tested for performance and documented.
